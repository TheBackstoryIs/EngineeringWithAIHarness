# Product design principles

- Begin with purpose: explain why the work matters before presenting machinery.
- Preserve agency: let people understand, choose, inspect, and recover.
- Show responsibility: consequences, owners, evidence, limitations, and approvals should be legible.
- Prefer familiar patterns and standard components before introducing novelty.
- Support flexible routes for different experience levels without fragmenting the truth.
- Pursue simplicity without minimalism: remove friction, not necessary context.
- Apply craft deliberately to typography, spacing, hierarchy, language, and transitions.
- Use delight with restraint, when it makes progress or understanding more human.
