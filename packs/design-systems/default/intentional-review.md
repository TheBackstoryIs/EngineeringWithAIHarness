# Intentional design and self-critique

Make deliberate visual and interaction choices that arise from the product's
purpose, users, evidence, and constraints. Avoid generic template composition.
Use established components where they satisfy the need; record why novelty is
necessary when they do not.

Before selection, self-critique the work for purpose, agency, responsibility,
familiarity, flexibility, simplicity, craft, meaningful delight, real content,
empty and error states, accessibility evidence, and unresolved deviations.
